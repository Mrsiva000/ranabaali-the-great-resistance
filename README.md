# 🔥 RANABAALI: The Great Resistance (3D WebGL Action RPG)

> *"Bobbili Ranabaali raa nee yakka!"* ⚔️🐎

An open-world 3D fan-made action-adventure game inspired by **Ranabaali** (starring **Vijay Deverakonda**[cite: 3]) and built for the browser with Three.js. Ride your dark warhorse across the scorched 1877 Rayalaseema badlands, ambush British Redcoat grain convoys, deliver food to starving villages, and invoke divine blessings!

---

## 🎮 How to Play & Controls

### 1. Movement & Riding
* **`W` / `A` / `S` / `D` or Arrow Keys**: Steer and ride your warhorse (or run on foot).
* **`SHIFT` Key**: Mount / Dismount your warhorse. (Dismounting allows you to hide inside green **Scrubland Bushes** for 100% stealth against Redcoats!).
* **`R` Key**: Perform the iconic **Poster Horse Rear-Up** against the giant Rayalaseema sun[cite: 3].

### 2. Combat & Divine Powers
* **`SPACEBAR` or Click**: Swing your curved **Kodavali sickle blade** to slash British Redcoats and Sir Theodore Hector's commanders[cite: 3]. 
  * *Killing an enemy triggers the cinematic **0.5s Slow-Mo Mass Kill-Cam**, blood/fire spark bursts, and roars the original dialogue audio!*
* **`E` Key (At 100% Faith)**: Unleash **Bhadra Mallanna's Wrath**—a divine shockwave that smotes nearby Redcoats and instantly surges Village Hope.

### 3. Core Objectives
1. **Raid British Grain Depots (Red Beacons / North & East):** Defeat Redcoat guards and loot stolen **Grain Sacks**. Carry up to 5 sacks at a time.
2. **Feed the Starving Village (South Green Beacon):** Ride south to **Jayamma's Village Granary** to deliver your grain and keep the **Village Hope Meter** from hitting 0%.
3. **Charge Your Faith (West Cyan Shrine):** Visit the **Bhadra Mallanna Temple** to recharge your divine wrath.

## 🚀 Running Locally

To run or test the game locally on your Mac/PC:
1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Ensure `index.html`, `ranabaalimp3.mp3`[cite: 4], and `ranabaali-vijay-devarakonda-movie-poster.jpg`[cite: 3] are in the same folder.
3. Open `index.html` in any modern web browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge).

## 🌐 Hosting & Live Deployment

This game runs entirely on static client-side WebGL and Web Audio. You can host it for free in under 60 seconds:

* **Vercel (Recommended):** Import your GitHub repository into [Vercel](https://vercel.com/new) and click **Deploy**.
* **GitHub Pages:** Go to your repository **Settings -> Pages**, select the `main` branch and `/ (root)` folder, and save.
